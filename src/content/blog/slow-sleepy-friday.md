<u>**1:54pm**</u>


Busy day today at work. I had meetings all morning, and just finished cooking and eating lunch. I had an iced coffee and a strawberry activia to push me through my meetings, all of them ran long. 


Lunch was steak! I had two filet mignon steaks defrosting in the fridge, so I cooked those up. I think I did okay. I had a pack of four, and my first two I used an airfryer and it came out underdone. I’m shooting for medium-rare. This time, I used the stove and I think I’m getting closer to what I want. I’m using a cheap non-stick pan, instead of cast-iron or stainless steel, but I’m not sure if that affects anything. I don’t have a thermometer either, so I just went by time. 3-4 minutes on each side. 5 minute rest. I thought it was fine… I ate them with a diet coke.

I cut up 2 persimmons for dessert. They were a little mushy, and I prefer them to be firmer usually. I think in the future, I need to try the types of persimmons so I can compare and figure out which one I like the most. The one’s I usually eat, I think are Fuyu persimmons, they’re short and round. 


My grandpa has a persimmon tree in his backyard in South Carolina. My parents went to visit him and my grandma over Halloweekend and got to pick the fruit and bring it home. I wish I had a fruit tree. I think it would be fun to be able to get fruit from the backyard. My mom, during the summer, has perilla plants, so when we decide to eat some k-bbq, I get to pick the leaves from the backyard. It’s fun. Before I used to grab the biggest leaves, because I thought those were the best and can hold the most meat, but my mom says the smaller leaves are better because they have more flavor and a better texture. She’s right. I think it’s similar to how microgreens have that snappy bright taste and standard heads of greens are a bit more dull tasting and stringier. 


Anyways, my day so far has been good. I didn’t sleep early last night like I planned. According to Screen Time, I spent at least 3 hours and 48 minutes playing sudoku on my phone, sprawled on the couch with airplane mode on. I definitely played more than that, since I kept playing on my iPad when I finally got in bed. Combined, I must’ve played over 4 hours, I think I stayed up until 2am. I’m sleepy now. I’m going to try to resist sudoku today, so I don’t doom-sudoku again. There’s no way that much sudoku is good for me…. I might play 2-3 games though, I don’t think I can quit cold turkey.

<u>8:25pm</u>


I played a little sudoku and then napped after work, slept for just about an hour. I feel a lot better now. I woke up at 7, because my mom was calling me. She was waiting with my dad for my little brother to get dropped off. My brother is back from college for Thanksgiving. After the call, I decided to stay up instead of going back to sleep. I’ll wait to sleep at a more normal time.


I ate leftover steak with rice, edamame, 깍두기, and a fried egg. I drizzled some soy sauce too. It was good, it felt like a real meal because there was something green on my plate, it wasn’t just beige slop. Yum! I ate it with, of course, a diet coke, and I watched 나혼산 while eating. Now, I’m drinking some tea, 둥굴레차, and planning on finishing the episode. Right now, 둥굴레차, is my favorite tea.


I watch 나혼산 every Friday. When I used to live with my parents, I would watch every week with my mom. Now, I watch alone and I text my mom while I do. I’ve been falling behind lately, so I have a couple of episodes to catch up on.


I look forward to watching 나혼산 every Friday, to the point that I get kind of cranky if I miss an episode. I’m sorry to anybody who tries to make plans with me on a Friday. Episodes are just following celebrities who live alone (나 혼자 산다 means I Live Alone) live their life. Some episodes they travel abroad or do exciting things (e.g. compete in a triathlon, bungee jumping, performing at concerts), but usually they’re doing normal things like cooking, cleaning, working out, shopping, sometimes getting dinner with friends. 


I like to get ideas from guests I see on there. I got the idea to make paper lamps after seeing 던 make one on the show. It seemed really fun, and it was. Sometimes, I’ll take recipes or workout ideas too. It’s just nice to see so many different ways to spend a day. Even though they’re celebrities, I feel like most of them are pretty grounded, and after watching them challenge themselves like I do, they seem like peers. The episode I’m watching right now, he’s trying to challenge himself by going out to eat alone for the first time. He ended up anxiously scarfing down a slice of pizza and then ordering delivery so he can eat in his hotel room without feeling self-conscious. I promise the show is more fun than it sounds. I’m realizing it sounds boring, that’s probably why I like it. I don’t like when there’s too much going on.
