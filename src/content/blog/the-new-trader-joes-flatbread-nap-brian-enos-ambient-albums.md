<u>**5:51pm**</u>


I love Saturdays. I just like having free time. Friday nights, I’m still a little tired from work, and Sundays, I start thinking about things I need to plan for Monday, but Saturdays are just for me. 


It feels so nice to go to bed without setting an alarm, and just waking up when it feels right, or when my cat starts yelling at me. Today, I woke up a little before 9. I was pretty relieved to see the time, sometimes I sleep in until 11 or 12 and it feels like I lost most of the day. I ate an oatmeal with egg and drank an iced coffee. I continued reading Blowfish while I ate, I was reading it in bed before I fell asleep. I’m not sure how I feel about it yet, I’ve been trying to read it for a year now. I keep getting stuck right in the middle, and rereading the first half over and over.


Today, I went to Trader Joe’s. I walked there. The weather was nice today, the sun was out and it wasn’t too cold. I liked seeing the leaves fall from the trees. Sometimes, I feel like days run together when I don’t go outside, the weather’s always the same inside. I also stopped by Ross again, but there was nothing there. I must’ve been lucky on Wednesday.


Trader Joe’s is a little dangerous, because whenever I go shopping there, I end up buying more fun things than normal groceries. It’s just not as practical, but that’s not why I go. I like to pretend I’m a serious grocery shopper when I walk in, just getting the necessities, and then I get forget. I bought eggs, cucumbers, spring mix, broccoli, a bag of mandarins, a flatbread pizza, cauliflower gnocchi, strawberry mochi, black sesame mochi, soup dumplings, 파전, baked cheese crunchies, a set of three hand lotions, and a chocolate passport. I would’ve bought more but I restrained myself, mostly because I knew I had to carry everything back home... I definitely would’ve bought more if I was stronger. I love Trader Joe’s.

I took the bus home, I didn’t get off a stop early this time. I’m learning. When I got back, I cooked the flatbread for lunch. It was a burrata, prosciutto, and arugula flatbread. It was really good. I liked the creaminess of the burrata, but the arugula got stringy while baking. I’ve been craving a pizza and flatbread is close enough for me. I added some red pepper flakes and more fresh arugula on the top. I drank it with an elderberry lemon balm tea and had a mandarin with it too. I ate the whole thing. Yum!


Then I had a nap. I’m not sure if I was sleepy because I was tired or it was a food coma. It also could’ve been the tea, since there’s ashwagandha in it. I must’ve slept for 2-3 hours. I woke up and it was dark outside.


I ate 고구마 with 둥굴레차 and baked cheese crunchies and another mandarin. I burnt my tongue on the tea. There’s a piece of skin dangling on the roof of my mouth, and tingles on my tongue. I’m not sure what I should do now.

<u>**9:04pm**</u>


I’m waiting on my last load of laundry, 3 loads today. I ate a pack of chicken bites and more handfuls of baked cheese crunchies. I showered and got ready for bed. I feel sleepy again. I ate too many baked cheese crunchies so my stomach hurts, but just a dull pain, if you offered me some more food, I’d probably still take it. Marcy stole a bit of chicken from the counter, I pretended to be upset, but I don’t mind. This is her version of hunting.


I’ve been listening to a lot of ambient music lately, most by Brian Eno. I really liked Ambient 2, so I listened to Ambient 3 in the shower. I’m not sure if I like Ambient 3 as much. It sounds like overlapping ringtones. It’s layered and busy. It’s not bad, but it’s not what I’m looking for right now. I want something that sounds a little more vague. I like Ambient 2, my favorite song is ‘Wind in Lonely Fences’. There’s another one I like, and it’s called ‘2003 Toyota Corolla’, I think the title is so funny. The album, named 2003 Toyota Corolla, uses a picture of a car, I assume it’s a 2003 Toyota Corolla, for their cover art, and the artist’s name is 2003 Toyota Corolla. They must’ve been very inspired by the Toyota Corollas, the songs in the album are named after 2003-2010 Toyota Corollas. I like ambient music a lot. I like that the artists are spending time creating sounds that capture something nebulous. I like that they don’t follow a standard structure either, it feels like they’re being created instinctually and I like to hear the choices that are made when artists aren’t given any rules. It’s nice to have on in the background, even if sometimes it sounds like the hum of my dishwasher, I like that I can hear myself think.


Tomorrow, I’m going back to the suburbs to see my family again. I was just there last weekend, but now my brother is back from college, and my kid uncle (the oldest one) is visiting, so we’re getting dinner with everybody, my parents, my little brother, my cousin and my kid uncle (the youngest one) who both live in Chicago now, and my kid uncle (the oldest one) who’s visiting. It’s like an early Thanksgiving, since my cousin and both kid uncles are going to South Carolina for Thanksgiving.
