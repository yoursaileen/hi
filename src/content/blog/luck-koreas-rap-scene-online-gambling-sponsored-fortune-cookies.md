<u>**7:47am**</u>


_I’m on the train to work. Thursdays are busier and sometimes I have trouble finding a seat, but today I got one on the upper level, so I’m feeling lucky._


On days I go to the office, I have trouble getting up. I need to get up earlier than I do on work from home days, and I tend to snooze my alarm up until the last minute. My morning routine is a scramble. The adrenaline rush manages to gets me out the door in time, but I’ll leave things behind if I don’t pack the night before. I think I packed everything I need today because I’m lucky today.


I need a coffee. I usually buy one at the Starbucks next to my office. Until I get there, on the train, I’ll sleep some more, or I’ll listen to loud music until I get a cup. Today, I’m listening to J-Tong, a rapper from Busan. I heard of him for the first time when he was on season 12 of Show Me The Money, leading a team with Hukky Shibaseki. I’m a big fan of both now, but the show is weird.


Show Me the Money is a competition show, somewhat similar to The Voice, where rappers battle one another in teams. They audition to be a part of 1 of 4 teams led by rapper producer pairs. It’s a Korean show, with an international push in season 12, but most contestants that made it to the final rounds were Korean, with some rappers from Japan and Thailand (Milly, who already performed at Coachella at this point) and all teams were lead by Korean artists. 


It’s weird because a majority of the contestants are blatantly copying American rappers to the point of caricature, and nobody bats an eye. 


Had to leave for my stop. I’m sure I’ll continue on later.

It’s only my second day of writing in here, but it seems familiar to me. I’ve journaled, off and on, since I was a teen, so I’m used to writing about my day and my thoughts. The only differences are that, one, I’m not writing on paper and, two, there’s a slight off-chance that someone else could read this. This has changed my style in a way that I cannot yet categorize as good or bad. It’s easier to go back and make edits, so my spelling is better, but, I’ve been going back and making structural changes as well, rephrasing sentences and even deleting entire blocks of text. Is that good, or am I missing out on that rawer quality of journaling? Maybe I’m not documenting as truthfully as I could be, if I’m doing rewrites? It’s new to reread what I write, and I can’t help but tweak things when I do. I’ll reread and I’ll realize that my phrasing is awkward, or I’m not communicating exactly what I want to say. Maybe I’m sculpting my writing closer to what I mean to write. When I write on paper, I write in a scrawl, or sometimes I purposely write over my previous writing, such that the overlapped words are impossible to decipher. I find it embarassing to go back and read, and I rarely go through old journals. I’m thinking of burning them. I’m surprised I haven’t deleted all of what I wrote already.


Yesterday, I think I had a lot to chat about, but I don’t have as much on my mind today. I used to have pottery class on Thursdays, but I don’t for a while, it’s nice to have the extra time, I’m sleepy from waking up early and tired from work. I think I’ll try to head to bed early.
